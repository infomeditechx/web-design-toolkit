# Prompt: Landing Page

Use this prompt for a single, focused landing page (campaign page, product
launch, lead-gen page) rather than a full multi-page site.

---

Build a landing page. Use the `ui-ux-pro-max` skill and the `standards/`
in this repo to guide the design.

**Brief:**
- Offer/product being promoted: [what exactly]
- Single conversion goal: [e.g. book a call, start a trial, buy now, join
  waitlist — pick ONE primary goal]
- Traffic source: [e.g. paid ads, email campaign, organic — affects how
  much context the page needs to assume]
- Target audience: [who, and what they care about]
- Tone/personality: [e.g. urgent and bold, calm and trustworthy, premium]
- Tech stack: [e.g. HTML+Tailwind — default to HTML+Tailwind if unsure,
  since landing pages are often deployed standalone]
- Proof points available: [testimonials, logos, stats, reviews — or
  "none yet, use placeholders clearly marked as such"]

**What I want you to do:**

1. Use the `ui-ux-pro-max` skill's `landing` domain for page-structure and
   CTA-strategy recommendations matched to the goal and traffic source.
2. Use the `style`, `color`, and `typography` domains for a palette/style
   fit to the tone and audience.
3. Structure the page around ONE conversion goal — apply
   `standards/conversion-ux.md` strictly: one primary CTA style, minimal
   nav (or none) that could lead traffic away from the goal, trust
   signals near the decision point.
4. Apply `standards/accessibility.md`, `standards/responsive-design.md`,
   `standards/design-quality.md`, `standards/seo-basics.md`, and
   `standards/performance.md` — a landing page gets one shot at a visitor,
   so speed and mobile experience matter especially.
5. Before calling it done, run `workflows/pre-launch-checklist.md`.

If I haven't given you real proof points (testimonials, stats, logos),
clearly mark placeholders as placeholders rather than inventing
fabricated claims or fake customer names.
